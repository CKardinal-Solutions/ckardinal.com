Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how CKardinal LLC (“CKardinal,” “we,” “us”) handles information in connection with this website, our business services and our software products, including mobile apps (together, the “Services”). Where a product has its own privacy notice, that notice also applies.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include.
  • Business information. If you work with us, we keep the contact and project details needed to do that work.
  • Purchases. In-app purchases are processed by the app store (for example, Apple). We do not receive your full payment card details.

Information we do not collect

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.

Data in our apps

Our apps are designed to keep your content on your device where possible. If an app asks for access to features such as the camera, microphone or files, that access is used only to provide the feature you are using, and you can change it at any time in your device settings. Each app’s App Store listing describes its data practices.

How we use information

  • To respond to you and provide support.
  • To provide, maintain and improve the Services.
  • To meet legal, accounting and security obligations.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers who help us operate (such as email hosting or app stores), when required by law, or as part of a business transfer. We do not sell it.

Retention and security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law, and we use reasonable measures to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices and rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you by emailing us. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law.

Children

Our Services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows when it last changed.
